Spectre 26.2 Amethyst Bypass port

AmethystScanner adapts Spectre Client 26.2 AmethystBypassModule by 4ldenz,
licensed GPL-3.0-only. Bypass, Light and ANBS+ Scan methods and the optional
tracer are retained. The shared GeodeGlowScan also derives from Spectre.
The corresponding license is spectre-GPL-3.0.txt.

Donut Client supplies native rendering, settings, input and HUD integration.
Original Donut authorship and MIT notices are retained. The distribution
includes GPL components and is accompanied by its matching source artifact.
