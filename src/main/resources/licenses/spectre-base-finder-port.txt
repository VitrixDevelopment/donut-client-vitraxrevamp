Spectre 26.2 Sus Chunk Finder port

ChunkFinderScanner, ActivityClues, SuspectObjects, GeodeGlowScan and
BaseFinderPanel are adapted from Spectre Client 26.2 by 4ldenz and are
licensed GPL-3.0-only. The corresponding license is spectre-GPL-3.0.txt.

The scanner and look-to-inspect card are connected to Donut Client's native
GUI/render pipelines, existing Base Finder module, settings and shortcut.
Original Donut authorship and MIT notices are retained. The distribution
includes GPL components and is accompanied by its matching source artifact.
