Spectre 26.2 workspace visual port

Workspace layout, widgets, module-array-list HUD and procedural ghost artwork are adapted from
Spectre Client 26.2, credited by its package metadata to 4ldenz and licensed
GPL-3.0-only. The license text accompanies this notice as spectre-GPL-3.0.txt.

The presentation is adapted to Minecraft's native GUI renderer and connected
to Donut Client's existing module controllers and settings. Original Donut
authorship and the original MIT notice are retained. This distribution includes
GPL components and is supplied with the corresponding source artifact.
