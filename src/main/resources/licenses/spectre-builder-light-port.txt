Spectre 26.2 AutoBuild and Light Debug port

The schematic model/reader, BuilderMaterials, MaterialPlan, SupplyDepotPlan,
BuildJobStore, CreativeBuilder and GroundNavigator are adapted from the
supplied Spectre Client 26.2 source by 4ldenz, licensed GPL-3.0-only.
Native BuilderBridge and BuilderPlacement integrate those tools with Donut.

LightDebugScanner adapts LightEspModule, including its denoise algorithm and
light-level colors. LightSnapshot and SessionWorker derive from Spectre.
Only Light Debug is included in this port; Player Bypass and Prime Chunk
Finder are not part of it. Rendering, preferences and input use Donut's APIs.

The corresponding license is spectre-GPL-3.0.txt. Original Donut authorship
and MIT notices are retained. The combined distribution includes GPL
components and is accompanied by its matching source artifact.
